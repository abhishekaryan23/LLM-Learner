Built on how memory actually works

Most courses optimise for how learning feels. This one optimises for what you'll still remember in a month. Every lesson uses these research-backed techniques:

Pre-testing

Two warm-up guesses before each lesson prime your attention, even when you're wrong.

Richland, Kornell & Kao, 2009

Retrieval practice

Quizzes and "explain it back" boxes make you pull ideas out of memory, which strengthens them far more than re-reading.

Roediger & Karpicke, 2006

Spaced repetition

A built-in review deck brings each idea back after about 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days, right before you'd forget.

Cepeda et al., 2006

Interleaving

Reviews mix topics, so you practise choosing the right tool, not just repeating one.

Rohrer & Taylor, 2007

Dual coding & segmenting

Animations pair pictures with short captions, and you control the pace, one step at a time.

Paivio; Mayer, Multimedia Learning

Elaboration & calibration

ELI5 analogies, "why does this work?" prompts, worked → faded examples, and confidence ratings on every quiz.

Dunlosky et al., 2013
