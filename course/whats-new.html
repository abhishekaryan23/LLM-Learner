Field notes

What's new

Every week a research pipeline scans new papers and lab releases. The few that matter most are verified against their sources and written up here as two-minute notes, each linked to the lesson it updates.

How this page stays current

  1. Every Monday an automated job collects the week's papers from arXiv (language, learning, vision and audio) and posts from major AI labs, and scores them against the course's topics.
  2. A research pass reads the top candidates and checks each claim against the original paper or announcement. Only significant, verified items become field notes.
  3. A person approves every update before it goes live. Lessons that need revising get flagged, and their "Reviewed" date changes when they're updated.
